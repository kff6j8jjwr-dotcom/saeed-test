Karachi E-Challans: Why Enforcement Must Continue

By Saeed Rasheed · Karachi

The faceless e-challan system introduced in Karachi showed that impartial enforcement can improve road behaviour. Though technical errors and duplicate challans require a fair grievance process, they are reasons to improve the system—not to abandon it.

Consistency Creates Discipline

The system had a visible deterrent effect on helmet use, seat belts, traffic signals, lane discipline, and wrong-way driving. When enforcement becomes intermittent, it sends the message that violations will again be tolerated.

Traffic discipline requires transparent, fair, and uninterrupted enforcement. Karachi needs a continuous management system rather than short-term campaigns.
