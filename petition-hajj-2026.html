Constitutional Petition / 2026

Hajj 2024 Information & Transparency

Const. Petition No. CPD-1633/2026 · Petitioner: Saeed Rasheed

IN THE HIGH COURT OF SINDH AT KARACHI

Constitutional Regular Bench Matter under Article 199(1)(c) of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, 1973.

Saeed Rasheed, [personal identifiers, address and family information redacted], Petitioner, versus the Federation of Pakistan through the Federal Secretary, Ministry of Religious Affairs & Interfaith Harmony; the Joint Secretary (Hajj); the Deputy Secretary (Hajj Policy); and the Deputy Secretary (Hajj Operation), Respondents.

Constitutional petition

This petition concerns the right to information and transparency in connection with the Government Hajj Scheme 2024, including information requested about policy arrangements, agreements, expenditure, services, safety and compensation.

Preliminary objection: compliance

An earlier petition, CPD 1388/2025, was dismissed as premature with liberty to invoke the statutory right-to-information mechanism first. The Petitioner states that he complied by filing an RTI request dated 23 May 2025 and a complaint before the Pakistan Information Commission. The requested information had not been furnished after the statutory period.

Background and agreements

The petition refers to directions of the Supreme Court of Pakistan in CA Nos. 800-L, 801-L and 802-L of 2013, dated 27 August 2013, and CP Nos. 1270, 1308 and 1309 of 2014 and CMA No. 4094 of 2014, dated 21 July 2014. It states that the Hajj Policy Formulation Committee, headed by the Secretary of the Ministry of Religious Affairs & Interfaith Harmony, was constituted to formulate and recommend the Hajj policy for Cabinet approval.

It identifies arrangements said to be required for the Hajj season, including the Haji Affairs Arrangements Agreement, the United Agents Office agreement, a general transport contract, an additional-services agreement, and arrangements with the National Adillah Establishment. References to personal applications, banking details, family members, social-media links and correspondence have been redacted.

Information sought

Under Article 19-A, the Petitioner seeks a detailed breakdown of the 2024 Hajj package and associated arrangements, including:

  • return air-ticket costs and airline details per pilgrim;
  • food, accommodation and transportation costs per pilgrim;
  • medical expenses and arrangements during Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah, Jamarat and Haram;
  • information on Pakistani pilgrims who died or were hospitalised, and investigation reports;
  • compensation payable to legal heirs and related policy and procedure;
  • evidence and details concerning Qurbani arrangements; and
  • the precautions, insurance, compensation and claim procedures proposed for the 2025 and 2026 Hajj policies.

The petition also seeks the agreements and memoranda signed between the Government of Pakistan and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in relation to Hajj 2024, and refers to a legal notice requesting this information from the relevant Ministry officials.

Cause of action

The petition states that the cause of action accrued with the announcement of the Hajj Policy 2024, the Petitioner’s application under the Government Hajj Scheme, the alleged consequences of inadequate arrangements during the Hajj period, subsequent communications with the Respondents, and the legal notice. It is pleaded to be continuing.

Grounds

The grounds pleaded are: (a) violation of Article 19-A; (b) non-performance of statutory duty; (c) mala fide withholding of public records; and (d) the right to transparency in the use of public funds.

Prayer

It is respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may:

  • declare the non-disclosure of information illegal;
  • direct the Respondents to provide the information sought in the RTI request dated 23 May 2025;
  • direct the Pakistan Information Commission to decide the complaint within a fixed time;
  • direct disclosure of the Hajj-package expenditure, service arrangements and the other information listed above; and
  • grant any other relief that the Court considers fit and proper in the circumstances.
