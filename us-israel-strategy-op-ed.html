US–Israel Strategy 2025–2027: Media Narrative vs Strategic Reality

By Saeed Rasheed

This original op-ed examines the gap between media perception and strategic developments in the Middle East, with a focus on recent geopolitical shifts and their long-term implications.

Rather than viewing events solely as an unplanned escalation, the article considers whether increasing intelligence coordination, military positioning, and changing regional alliances point to a broader strategic framework. It invites readers to consider how this shift may reshape security, energy, and diplomacy across the region.
