Free Food Culture & Self-Reliance in Karachi

By Saeed Rasheed · Karachi

Free food programmes can be driven by genuine humanitarian concern, but unregulated distribution raises difficult questions about dependency, accountability, and whether support reaches those who genuinely cannot earn.

Compassion With Structure

The article argues for targeted assistance for disabled people, older people, widows, and others unable to work, while favouring subsidised food and eligibility checks for those who can. A modest payment can preserve dignity while still providing support.

From Relief to Opportunity

Transparent oversight of zakat and donations is essential. Investment in vocational training, small enterprises, industry, rehabilitation, and jobs offers a more lasting form of help. Feeding the hungry is noble; empowering people to support themselves is nation-building.
