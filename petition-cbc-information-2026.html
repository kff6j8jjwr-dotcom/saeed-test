Constitutional petition

This petition seeks enforcement of the fundamental right of access to information under Article 19-A of the Constitution and the Right of Access to Information Act, 2017.

1. Status of the petitioner

The Petitioner is a law-abiding citizen of Pakistan and a resident of Karachi, directly affected by the public funds and municipal services provided by Respondent No. 3, a public body funded through public taxation.

The Petitioner is entitled to enforce the fundamental rights guaranteed under Article 19-A (right of access to information), Article 4 (right to be treated in accordance with law), and Article 25 (equality before law).

2. Status of respondents

The Respondents are public authorities performing statutory functions under the Cantonments Act, 1924, and are bound to provide public information under Article 19-A of the Constitution and the Right of Access to Information Act, 2017.

3. Jurisdiction

This Honourable Court has jurisdiction under Article 199 because the Respondents are located and perform official functions within its territorial jurisdiction. Enforcement of fundamental rights falls within the constitutional jurisdiction of this Honourable Court.

4. Facts of the case

The Petitioner applied on 16 January 2026 to the Designated Information Officer, Cantonment Board Clifton, under section 11 of the Right of Access to Information Act, 2017, requesting certified information for the financial years 2022–2023, 2023–2024 and 2024–2025 concerning:

  • annual budgets and revenue collection;
  • expenditure details, audited accounts and financial-position statements;
  • water-supply and tanker revenue; and
  • development expenditure and contractor details.

A copy of the application is annexed as Annexure A. Under section 14 of the Act, the Respondents were required to provide the requested information within ten working days. Despite expiry of that period, the Respondents failed, refused and neglected to provide it. The refusal is illegal, unconstitutional, arbitrary and without lawful justification.

5. Grounds

A. Violation of Article 19-A. Article 19-A guarantees every citizen a fundamental right to access public information held by public bodies. The refusal violates that constitutional guarantee.

B. Violation of the Right of Access to Information Act, 2017. Under sections 4, 7, 8, 11 and 14, the Respondents are legally bound to provide financial and public records.

C. Public authority. The Cantonment Board is funded through public taxation, fees and government funds and is legally required to maintain transparency.

D. Public interest. The information concerns public funds, taxation, services and municipal financial transparency, and cannot be withheld.

E. Arbitrary and illegal conduct. The failure to provide information constitutes misuse of authority and violates constitutional and statutory obligations.

6. Cause of action

The cause of action arose on 16 January 2026, when the application was submitted, and continues because of the alleged refusal.

7. Prayer

It is respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may:

  • declare that the Petitioner is entitled to the requested information under Article 19-A and the Right of Access to Information Act, 2017;
  • direct the Respondents to provide certified copies of the requested budgets, revenue collection, expenditure statements, audited accounts, and water-supply and tanker-revenue records within a time fixed by the Court;
  • declare the refusal illegal and unconstitutional;
  • direct compliance with the Right of Access to Information Act, 2017; and
  • grant any other just and proper relief.

8. Interim relief, affidavit and annexures

Pending final disposal, the Respondents may be directed to preserve all financial records to prevent destruction or concealment. An affidavit in support is filed. The annexures include the information request dated 16 January 2026, responses from the CBC office, and proof of submission where available.
